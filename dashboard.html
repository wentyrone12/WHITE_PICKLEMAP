ABOUT PICKLEFINDER

How this works

1. Find

Use the map, search, and filters to discover pickleball courts anywhere in the Philippines. Type a city or place and press Enter to jump there.

2. Verify

Venue addresses, rates, and hours can change. Open the venue details or Directions link before travelling.

3. Share

Signed-in players can post a community availability report. Recent reports appear on court cards.

Court locations are loaded live from OpenStreetMap (community-mapped, all over the Philippines) plus a curated Cotabato City directory. They are not an official registry, so please verify before travelling.
